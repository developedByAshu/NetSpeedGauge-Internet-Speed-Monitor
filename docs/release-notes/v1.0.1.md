Fixes and a few new settings.

- The speed stays on the taskbar while Quick Settings is open or the taskbar is active. It used to disappear behind it
- Choose what hover and click do: nothing, the Usage card or the Usage window. Hover closes when the mouse leaves, click stays until you click again or click elsewhere
- Icons, Background and Colour by speed now have a switch on their heading that hides what you do not use
- Text moved up under Theme in Appearance
- Light theme has clearer sections and controls

To update, choose **Exit**, then replace your old file with the new one. Your settings and usage stay as they are.

Take `NetSpeedGauge-x64.exe`, or `NetSpeedGauge-arm64.exe` on a Windows on ARM PC.

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the program is not code signed yet. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
