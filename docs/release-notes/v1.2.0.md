NetSpeedGauge now looks and moves like part of your system, on both Windows and Mac.

- Windows: the Usage card and the menu slide up from behind the taskbar, like Windows' own panels
- Windows: the speed numbers highlight when you hover over them and stay highlighted while the card or menu is open
- Mac: the menu bar item stays selected while its panel is open, and the panel opens and closes like other Mac menu bar items
- Mac: fixed the first click being lost after closing a panel, and Settings opening without focus

**Windows:** take `NetSpeedGauge-x64.exe`, or `NetSpeedGauge-arm64.exe` on a Windows on ARM PC. To update, choose **Exit**, then replace your old file with the new one. Your settings and usage stay as they are. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the program is not code signed yet. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Mac:** take `NetSpeedGauge-mac-arm64.zip`, unzip it and move the app to Applications. There is no version for Intel Macs. macOS blocks it the first time because the app is not signed by Apple. Open **System Settings**, **Privacy & Security**, choose **Open Anyway**, then open it again.
